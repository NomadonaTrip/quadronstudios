Trader Moni

The app's core mission is to impart essential skills and knowledge on effectively onboarding new merchants. To achieve this, we painstakingly recreated the vibrant atmospheres of two iconic Nigerian markets, Obalende and Wuse, within our virtual platform.

Harnessing the power of the Oculus Quest, our exceptional team achieved a remarkable feat by optimizing the app to run seamlessly at an impressive rate of over 72 frames per second. This ensures an unparalleled and immersive experience on the lightweight VR headset, allowing trainees to navigate through the virtual markets with stunning realism and seamless interaction.

By immersing our recruits in lifelike scenarios, they gain invaluable practical experience, honing their skills and decision-making abilities in a risk-free environment. The virtual replication of Obalende and Wuse markets provides an authentic backdrop for learning the intricacies of merchant onboarding.
